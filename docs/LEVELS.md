# Levels

**Round 4 rules (owner test of 0.3.0).** The jump apex is ~230 px and a quick tap rises ~172 px, so a required plain step-up is at most 170 px. Difficulty comes from enemies, timing and hazards, never from pixel-perfect jumps. A pit that needs DRISHTI is either warned by a prompt or close to a lamp. An arrow at the screen edge points to the gate. Two deaths within 400 px show the sign of the mantra that crosses that stretch. Chapter 1 guards have 2 HP (one JWALA), later guards 3. Brutes (5 HP, fire from the front only staggers them) appear from chapter 2 on.

Times are for a skilled player who knows the level. The owner cleared the old levels in 23-33 s, so they were too easy. Each level follows teach, then test, then twist. Checkpoints come every 25-35 s of play. Respawns never land in an archer's range (650 px) or within 200 px of a soldier's patrol. Two embers per level are hard to reach: they need UDAAN height, DRISHTI on a hidden stone, or TARANG on a cracked wall. `tests/test_config.gd` checks 2D reachability (jump, UDAAN and SETU ramps, using only taught mantras) and these spacing rules.

**Round 5 rules (owner played 0.4.0 end to end in about 30 min).** 15 levels, 5 per chapter. Hearts last the whole level: a hit or a pit costs one, a pit sends you to the last lamp, nothing refills them except one rare heart pickup per level (high on a lone stone or behind a smoke wall), and losing the last heart restarts the level ("Lives lost"). UDAAN now leaps ~368 px (1.6x a jump) and glides 1.5 s (sinks 80 px/s, steerable), so the 2D test allows UDAAN step-ups to 540 px and counts the glide; TARANG-only cracked walls are 640 px tall. False stones waver every 2.4 s for 0.6 s (deeper fade plus a sideways shiver; the owner fell 3x at c2_l3 x 1800-2200).

### New maya (illusion) mechanics
Each is met first on safe ground under a prompt (`tests/test_config.gd` checks a prompt starts before the first one, within 1500 px).
- **Phantom walls** (`phantom_walls: [x, base_y, height]`, 110 px wide, `src/game/phantom_wall.gd`): a rock wall with no body. Walk through. It hides the honest stair, secret alcoves and hearts. Tell: a heat-haze ripple every 3 s. DRISHTI shows it thin and red-edged. Once walked through it stays thin. Taught in c1_l4 (no DRISHTI needed).
- **False gates** (`false_gates: [[x, y]]`, `src/game/false_gate.gd`): a copy of the exit door. Step in and it blows apart as smoke while the Smoke King laughs. Tell: its light stutters and the door ripples. Taught in c1_l5.
- **Mirage guards** (enemy `"maya": "mirage"`, soldiers and brutes only): smoke. Their spears and slams do nothing; a strike, a bolt or DRISHTI bursts them (no prana drop). Taught in c2_l4.
- **Veiled guards** (enemy `"maya": "veiled"`, any kind): real but invisible except a faint shimmer, the wind-up glint and the wind-up sound. DRISHTI shows them while it lasts; a hit tears the veil for good. Taught in c2_l4.
- **Veiled gate** (`"goal_veiled": true`): the real exit is a faint shimmer (and the goal arrow is hidden) until DRISHTI shows it or the hero comes within 160 px. Taught in c2_l5 next to a false gate ("One door lies. One hides.").
- **False floor over thorns** (data only: `maya_false` at floor height over a spiked hollow 140 px down, hidden stones above): stepping on the lie costs a heart, not a life; the hollow is climbable.
- **Thorn hollows** (data only: a spiked floor 170 px down): the UDAAN glide crosses them; falling in costs a heart.

## Chapter 1: The Ash Pits
**c1_l1 The Ash Pits** (difficulty 2, ~40 s). Tutorial for movement, SETU, CHHAYA, JWALA and prana orbs. Retuned: a third guard holds the top ledge before the gate, gaps are tighter, checkpoints dropped from 4 to 3, and every prompt is 6 words or fewer. Vertical: the stair steps are 90 px or less.

**c1_l2 The Chain Ladder** (3, ~45 s). Teaches UDAAN on a 210 px cliff, which a SETU ramp can also climb. Retuned: adds thorn spikes (first at x=700, jumpable, with an ember over them), a fifth guard near the gate, a wider SETU pit (440 px), 3 checkpoints, and two hard embers (one mid-pit, one UDAAN-high).

**c1_l3 The Burning Steps** (4, ~70 s). New ideas: **crumbling ledges** and **archers**. Crumbles are taught as stairs over solid ground, then tested as a chain over a pit, then twisted into a descending chain with an archer covering the landing. The burning steps are a 120 px staircase with spikes on each tread, under archer fire. The finale is a barrier, a 4-HP soldier, crumbles, and an archer at the gate. The cracked-wall secret sits behind the spawn.

**c1_l4 The Hollow Walls** (5, 14,100 px, target 3-4 min first play). New: **phantom walls**, and the UDAAN **glide** over thorn hollows. Lesson wall on flat ground ("Some walls are only smoke.") → crumble chain over a pit → guard and thorns → 800 px thorn hollow ("Leap, then glide over thorns.") with an ember mid-air → guard covered by an archer → a 3-step stair whose last step is walled off by smoke → top ledge guard, with the heart on a lone stone 350 px up behind a smoke wall → crumbles down to a 540 px SETU pit ("Bridge the long dark.") → two guards and a thorn wall (JWALA) → a second thorn hollow → crumble climb to the gate ledge with a guard, an archer and a last smoke wall. 4 lamps, 11 embers, 8 enemies.

**c1_l5 The False Door** (6, 12,600 px). New: **false gates**; chapter 1 finale. Lesson door on flat ground ("Not every door is true.") and a thorn wall → guard post (two guards, perched archer) → a 360 px UDAAN cliff → crumbles down → 820 px thorn hollow → two guards and thorns → SETU pit under an archer → the twist: a raised ledge ends in a door that is smoke beside a drop ("Doors lie. Watch the light."); the true gate is on the low ground past it, behind a smoke wall that also hides the heart on a high stone. 4 lamps, 11 embers.

## Chapter 2: Mirror Marsh
**c2_l1 Mirror Marsh** (4, ~55 s). Teaches DRISHTI. Round 4: the first pit has no false stone (it is a plain invisible-stepping-stone lesson) and the triangle prompt runs up to its edge. Retuned: a third maya pit with a twist (a false stone at ground height posing as the far bank), the first archer in the marsh, a guard at the gate, and 3 checkpoints. The hidden steps rise 80 px or less.

**c2_l2 Drowned Lanterns** (5, ~80 s). New idea: **moving platforms**. The first one is a plain shuttle, then an elevator under an archer. Then a mover combined with hidden stones, then a three-lantern chain (vertical, horizontal, diagonal) with a false bait stone. The twist is a long mover ending at a stone and an elevator to the gate. SETU works as a costly fallback on every crossing. Round 4 (owner deaths at x 6400-9500): the lantern chain has a real stone between the movers (6810), the long mover lost its false stones and ends on a real stone (9250), new lamps at 5940 and 9700, and the gate guard is a brute.

**c2_l3 The Lying Bridge** (7, ~90 s). Round 4 (11 owner deaths at x 1800-2600): the bridge is four honest stones with 160 px hops; the false stones sit in the gaps as bait and waver faintly every few seconds. A lamp (1420) and a "Some stones lie." prompt come before it, and "The floor ahead lies." warns of the false floor. The finale is four honest descending stones with two jets under archer fire. The 4-HP soldier is a brute. Original notes: New idea: **flame jets**. They are taught on safe ground, then a pair is staggered. The bridge alternates real stones with false ones, the hidden real stones sit off-line, and a jet burns on the middle stone. Then a jet "wave" corridor, an archer duel across a maya pit, and a 500 px false floor hiding a hidden staircase. That staircase can only be crossed with DRISHTI, so an orb sits just before it. The finale is a descending maya bridge with a jet stone and an archer at the gate. It has 4 checkpoints and 5 orbs.

**c2_l4 Hall of Mirages** (7, 13,200 px). New: **mirage** and **veiled** guards. Mirage lesson ("Some guards are only smoke." + triangle) → 3-stone hidden bridge → veiled lesson ("Unseen spears. Draw the triangle.") → 3-jet run → mover → the hall: two mirages, a real guard, a veiled guard and an archer on a stone → a lying bridge (honest stones, false bait) → a false floor over thorns with hidden stones above → a brute and his mirage twin, the heart on a hidden stone 330 px up, an archer at the gate. 4 lamps, 11 embers, 10 enemies.

**c2_l5 The Drowned Shrine** (8, 15,100 px). New: **veiled gate** with a false gate as bait. A hidden stair rising 200 px ("The steps rise unseen.") → a veiled guard and a mirage → an elevator down to a 4-jet run → lying bridge, a guard and a veiled archer → thorn hollow and a brute → a hidden bridge with a false floor laid over it as bait → a mirage and a veiled guard → the shrine: a false door on a high stone, the heart on the next stone, and the true gate veiled on the low ground ("One door lies. One hides."). 4 lamps, 11 embers.

## Chapter 3: The Palace Walls
**c3_l1 Outer Gate** (6, ~85 s). Round 4 (owner deaths at x 6600 and 9400): the inner moat has an honest stone (6590) instead of a false/hidden pair, the final climb rests on a solid stone (9240) between crumbles, and the captain is a brute. Original notes: Combines what came before, with tougher 4-5 HP soldiers. The route runs moat shuttle → gatehouse (perched archer, 4-HP soldier, gate barrier) → crumble climb up the wall → twin braziers on the wall top → courtyard brawl (two soldiers and a balcony archer) → inner moat (hidden stone, elevator, jet stone, crumble) → a 5-HP captain under two stacked archers → a crumble climb to the inner arch. The cracked-wall secret sits behind the spawn (440 px tall, so UDAAN cannot clear it).

**c3_l2 Gardens of Ash** (8, ~95 s). Round 4 (owner deaths at x 3200-3500): the stone before the garden mover is solid (3100), so you can wait for it; a lamp on the terraces (1450); the 5-HP soldier is a brute. Original notes: Fountains (jets) and terraces. The route runs 120 px terraces with spikes under an archer → a crumble-crumble-mover-crumble garden, where you must read the mover's timing before committing → a hedge barrier → a 500 px false "pool" → a four-jet wave → an elevator, crumble and ledge climb to a 5-HP soldier and an archer → a final hidden stone, crumble and two movers, under archer fire.

**c3_l3 The Smoke Stair** (9, ~105 s). Round 4: a full jump (230 px) now reaches the next tier, a quick tap (172 px) does not; the top captain is a brute. A vertical climb, 1080 px up a tower. Six tiers are stacked 180 px apart. That was above the old jump height (160 px) and above a soldier's 160 px sight band, so you climb only at the connectors: crumble steps, elevators over the right-hand abyss, an UDAAN or SETU ramp at the left end of tier 2, and a crumble step on tier 4. A fall usually costs one tier, not a death. Tier 3 has a crumble gap and tier 5 a moving-platform gap. Jets sit only where nothing hangs above them (220 px flames). The top floor has a thorn door, a 5-HP captain and two archers. It has 5 checkpoints.

**c3_l4 The Mirror Court** (9, 14,700 px). Everything mixed. A mirage at the spawn → crumble climb into a court (4-HP guard, mirage, veiled guard) → the court ends in a smoke wall over a false floor on thorns → two jets and an archer → a mover, one hidden stone, and a veiled brute with a 4-HP guard → thorn hollow → SETU pit down to a mirage, a brute and a veiled archer → a false gate at the foot of a hidden stair that climbs 240 px to the real gate (heart on a stone 310 px over the top ledge, 5-HP captain). 4 lamps, 11 embers, 11 enemies.

**c3_l5 Throne of Smoke** (10, 18,300 px, the longest). The finale. Thorn wall → 4-jet gatehouse → 4-HP guard, veiled guard, perched archer → lying bridge → false floor on thorns → a brute and his mirage → a smoke wall hiding the crumble stair → a 300 px UDAAN cliff, veiled guard, jet → diagonal mover down → a baited hidden bridge over the throne moat → mirage, 5-HP captain, veiled archer, heart on a lone stone above → thorn hollow → the throne hall: a veiled brute and a brute, the false throne door ("The throne door is smoke."), a smoke wall, and the veiled gate. 5 lamps, 11 embers, 11 enemies.

## Assumptions for the new entities (the levels depend on them)
- **Archer:** bolts fly at about 280 px/s toward where the hero was when fired. They pass through one-way ledges and stop at solid ground. Range is a straight-line distance (Euclidean) of 650 px. The first shot comes about 0.8 s after the hero enters range.
- **Jets:** each runs on a level clock (`t + offset`), so staggered offsets stay in phase. The 40 px hitbox rises 220 px. Show a short flicker telegraph before each burn. Two jets 150 px apart leave a safe 110 px pocket between them. Jets hurt only the hero.
- **Moving:** the platform starts at (x, y) and ping-pongs to (x+dx, y+dy). It is one-way and carries the hero.
- **Crumble:** only the hero triggers it. It returns after 3 s.
- **Spikes:** soldiers ignore them, and the hit grants the usual 1.2 s of invulnerability.
- **Soldiers** chase up to 650 px past their patrol but stop at any drop deeper than 140 px, so they stay on their platform (c3_l3 tiers). They hop up ledges of 130 px or less. Their patrol must lie on one surface.
- Defeated enemies stay dead after a respawn.
