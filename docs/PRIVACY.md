# Tamra: Privacy Policy

Published at https://iiakshat.github.io/tamra-privacy/ (public repo iiakshat/tamra-privacy, since private repos cannot use free Pages). Not legal advice; have it read once by someone who knows India's DPDP Act before the production launch.

**Tamra** is a single-player offline game by Akshat, an independent developer in India. Contact: jakshat569@gmail.com. Last updated: 28 October 2026.

## The short version
- The game works fully offline. There is no account, and the game never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
- The first time you open the game it asks whether you want to share **anonymous play data**. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your mind at any time in **Settings > Share play data**.
- This data is never used for advertising and is not sold.

## What is collected, only if you say yes
- **Play events:** for example which level you started, finished or failed, how long it took, which mantras you used, and settings such as pad size.
- **Crash reports and performance:** when the game crashes, the technical details of the crash and which level you were on.
- **Device facts:** phone model, Android version, screen size and graphics chip, so problems on particular phones can be fixed.
- **A random app ID** that Firebase creates for this install. It is not linked to your name or Google account and is reset when you uninstall the game.

This is collected and processed with Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). Google's own privacy terms apply to its processing: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## What is not collected
Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, or anything from other apps. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are switched off.

## Where your game progress lives
Your save (levels, settings, drawn signs) stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Your choices
- Switch sharing off any time in Settings. Collection stops immediately.
- To ask for data from your install to be deleted, email jakshat569@gmail.com with your phone model and roughly when you played. The data is anonymous, so it may not be findable without those details.

## Children
Tamra is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has shared data, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes
If this policy changes in a way that matters, the game will ask you again before sharing anything.
