## The short version
- The game works fully offline. You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
- The first time you open the game it asks whether you want to share **anonymous play data**. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your mind any time in **Settings > Share play data**.
- We never use this data for ads, and we do not sell it.

## What is collected, only if you say yes
- **Play events:** for example which level you started, finished or failed, how long it took, which mantras you used, and settings such as pad size.
- **Crash reports and performance:** when the game crashes, the technical details of the crash, and which level you were on.
- **Device facts:** phone model, Android version, screen size and graphics chip, so we can fix problems on low-end phones.
- **A random app ID** that Firebase creates for this install. It is not linked to your name or your Google account, and it is reset if you uninstall.

This is collected with Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), which processes it for us. Google's own terms apply to its processing: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## What we do not collect
Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, exact or approximate location, advertising ID, or anything from other apps. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are switched off.

## Where your game progress lives
Your save (levels, settings, drawn signs) stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
