## Full description (4000 max)
Tamra is a hand-painted platformer where your finger is the weapon. Draw a sign on the screen and magic answers: a bridge of light across a chasm, a shadow that fools the guards, a bolt of flame.

Tamra is a young asura who refused the Smoke King's order to burn a sleeping village. Thrown into the Ash Pits, he climbs out with the old mantras his mother taught him and the power of maya, illusion.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• Draw to cast. Eight mantras, each one a sign you draw with your finger. Time slows while you draw, so casting in mid-jump is fair. You can even record your own sign for any shape mantra.
• Trick, don't just fight. Illusions, false floors, phantom walls and hidden stairs mean the clever answer is not always the staff.
• A story told in Hindi. Full Hindi voice-over with English subtitles, and a little Sanskrit for the old mantras.
• 15 levels across three chapters: the Ash Pits, Mirror Marsh and the Palace Walls.
• Made to be fair. Plays fully offline. No forced ads, no waiting timers, generous checkpoints.
• Built for your thumbs. Choose the size of the on-screen pads and a left-handed layout in Settings.
• A painted shadow-puppet look, with leather-puppet heroes and painted skies.

COME BACK TOMORROW
A daily blessing, daily vows and a daily trial: a level you have cleared, with a twist.

YOUR DATA
The game asks once whether you would like to share anonymous play data to help improve it. Say no and nothing is sent. You can change your mind any time in Settings.

Tamra is an original story inspired by Indian epic traditions. All characters and names are original.
